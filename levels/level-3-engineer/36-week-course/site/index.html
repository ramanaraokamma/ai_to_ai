⚠ Structural warning 1 — do not rush Week 12

Everything from Week 15 to Week 27 stands on the idea that you can measure how steep a curve is at one point by nudging and dividing. A student who leaves Week 12 without having done that arithmetic with their own pencil will experience the rest of the year as memorising incantations. If Week 12 needs two weeks, take two weeks and compress Week 5 instead.

⚠ Structural warning 2 — never teach Weeks 18 and 20 together

Week 18 is four gradient arrays computed by hand — genuinely hard, genuinely tiring, and the proudest moment of the year. Week 20 is PyTorch producing all four of them in one line. That gap needs the pride to sit in it for seven days, or the lesson becomes “so that was pointless then”. It is the only reason loss.backward() will ever mean anything.
