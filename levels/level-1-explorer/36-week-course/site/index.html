Level 1 · Explorer · The 36-Week Course

One school year to build a real AI — and prove how good it isn’t.

Thirty-six classes of 60–75 minutes for one 6th grader, taught by an adult who knows nothing about AI. No coding, no maths beyond fractions and percentages, no preparation beyond the twenty minutes before class.

“AI is a system that turns examples into a guessing machine.”
